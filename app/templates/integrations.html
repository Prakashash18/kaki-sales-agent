Before connecting a live mailbox

Use durable PostgreSQL storage and tenant-aware authentication. OAuth secrets and token encryption keys must be supplied through Render's protected environment variables, never GitHub. Connection testing is read-only. This pilot must use synthetic or explicitly authorised business data. Incoming mail sync is manual in this release; push notifications, renewal, and reply sending need additional production integration.
