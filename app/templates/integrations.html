Before connecting a live mailbox

Use durable PostgreSQL storage and tenant-aware authentication. OAuth secrets and token encryption keys must be supplied through Render's protected environment variables, never GitHub. This prototype is not approved for customer data. Incoming mail sync is manual in this release; push notifications, renewal, and reply sending need additional production integration.
